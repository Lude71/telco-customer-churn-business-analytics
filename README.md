# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Business Analytics Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a Business Analytics Case Study focused on understanding why customers leave a telecommunications company (customer churn). Using the Telco Customer Churn Dataset, I performed data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualization, and business analysis to identify the key drivers of customer churn and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

The project demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecommunications companies. Losing customers reduces revenue, increases customer acquisition costs, and negatively impacts long-term business growth.

This project answers the following business questions:

- Why are customers leaving the company?
- Which customer groups are at the highest risk of churn?
- Which business factors contribute most to customer churn?
- What strategies can improve customer retention?

Multiple visualizations were created to identify trends, patterns, and customer behaviors.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

The analysis revealed several important factors influencing customer churn:

- Approximately **26.58%** of customers had churned.
- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** experienced the highest churn.
- Customers who churned paid **higher monthly charges** on average.
- Customers with **shorter tenure** were significantly more likely to leave.
- **Fiber optic** customers recorded the highest churn among internet service types.
- Customers using **Electronic check** had the highest churn among payment methods.
- Customers without **Technical Support** were much more likely to churn.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were proposed:

- Encourage customers to upgrade to long-term contracts.
- Improve onboarding for new customers.
- Review pricing strategies for high-risk customers.
- Improve customer experience for Fiber optic subscribers.
- Promote automatic payment methods.
- Expand technical support services to improve customer retention.
